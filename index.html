Uyen Doan

I am a PhD Candidate at the Complex Human Data Hub, University of Melbourne. I study the relationship between memory and belief through Bayesian and clinical frameworks.

I am particularly interested in memory narratives of personally experienced past events (autoiographical memories). I use large cohort datasets, natural language processing tools, experimental paradigms in semantic comprehension and associative learning, and computational modelling to study how and why differences in specificity of memory details might emerge as well as its implications.
